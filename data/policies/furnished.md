# Furnished and all-inclusive rentals

## All-inclusive rent
For furnished flats let at an all-inclusive rent (Warmmiete inklusive), the stated rent covers utilities, heating, electricity and internet. There is no separate utility statement.

## Fixed terms
Furnished flats are often let for a fixed term. The end date is stated in the lease, and the lease ends on that date without notice.

## Registration
Tenants must register their residence (Anmeldung) within two weeks of moving in. The landlord provides the confirmation of residence (Wohnungsgeberbestätigung).
