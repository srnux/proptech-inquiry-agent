# Deposit (Kaution)

## Amount
For residential rentals the deposit is at most three months' net cold rent (Nettokaltmiete). Utilities are not included in that calculation. Each listing states the deposit that applies to it.

## Payment in instalments
Tenants may pay the deposit in three equal monthly instalments. The first instalment is due at the start of the lease.

## Return of the deposit
The deposit is returned after the tenant moves out, once the handover protocol is signed and any open utility statement is settled. Part of it can be held back until the final utility statement is issued.
