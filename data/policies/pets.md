# Pets

## Small animals
Small animals kept in cages or tanks, such as hamsters, fish or birds, are allowed in every rental.

## Dogs and cats
Dogs and cats need the landlord's consent. "On request" in a listing means the landlord decides case by case after the inquiry; it is not a confirmation. "No" means the building rules exclude them.

## How to ask
Mention the type, size and number of animals in the inquiry. Our letting team asks the landlord and replies with a decision.
