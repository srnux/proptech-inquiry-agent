# Viewings

## How viewings are arranged
Viewings are arranged only by our letting and sales team, by email or phone. The online assistant cannot book, confirm or change viewing appointments.

## What to bring
Bring an ID document. For rentals, a proof of income and a tenant self-disclosure form are requested after the viewing, not before.

## Energy certificate
The energy certificate is shown at the viewing at the latest, and a copy is handed over when the contract is signed.
