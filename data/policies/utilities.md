# Utilities (Nebenkosten)

## What the monthly figure means
The utilities figure in a listing is a monthly advance payment on top of the cold rent. It usually covers water, waste collection, building insurance, cleaning of shared areas and property tax. Whether heating is included is stated in each listing.

## Annual statement
Once a year the tenant receives a utility statement (Betriebskostenabrechnung) that compares the advance payments with the actual costs. It must be issued within twelve months after the end of the billing period. The result is either a refund or an additional payment.

## Electricity and internet
Household electricity and internet are contracted by the tenant directly and are not part of the utilities, unless a listing says the rent is all-inclusive.
