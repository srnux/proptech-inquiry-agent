# Graduated rent (Staffelmiete)

## How it works
With a graduated rent the future rent increases are written into the lease in advance, either as amounts or as dates with new rents. Each step must be at least one year after the previous one.

## What it excludes
During a graduated rent lease the landlord cannot raise the rent to the local comparative rent (Mietspiegel) in between steps. Increases after modernisation are also excluded.
