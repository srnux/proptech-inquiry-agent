# Buying a condominium

## Service charge (Hausgeld)
Owners of a condominium pay a monthly service charge (Hausgeld) to the owners' association. It covers running costs of the building and a contribution to the maintenance reserve (Erhaltungsrücklage). Part of it can be passed on to a tenant if the flat is let.

## Special levies
When the maintenance reserve does not cover a decided repair, the owners' association can charge a special levy (Sonderumlage). Until the association has decided the final amount, any figure given is an estimate.

## Documents before purchase
Before signing, buyers receive the minutes of the last owners' meetings, the current economic plan and the declaration of division (Teilungserklärung).
